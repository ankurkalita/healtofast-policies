# Healthofast Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 26, 2026**

Healthofast ("Healthofast", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Healthofast handles information when you use the Healthofast app.

## 1. Information We Collect

Healthofast is designed to support offline use.

- You can use the app without creating an account or logging in.
- Healthofast does **not intentionally collect sensitive personal information**.
- When you choose to log in, information associated with your account and app data may be synchronized so that your data can be available across supported sessions or devices.
- We collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide the app's login, synchronization, and related functionality.

## 2. Offline Use

Healthofast can be used offline. When you use the app offline, your app data can remain stored locally on your device and does not need to be transmitted to our servers solely for you to use the offline features.

If you subsequently log in or use synchronization features, relevant app data may be transmitted to our servers to provide synchronization.

## 3. Data Storage

Healthofast data that is stored on our servers is stored in **India**.

Data stored locally on your device is subject to the security and privacy controls of your device and operating system.

## 4. How We Use Information

We may use information associated with your use of Healthofast to:

- provide and maintain the app;
- authenticate users who choose to log in;
- synchronize app data;
- protect the service from misuse; and
- troubleshoot and improve the app.

We do not use information for purposes incompatible with providing and improving Healthofast.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may disclose information only when reasonably necessary to:

- provide or maintain Healthofast services;
- comply with applicable law or a valid legal process; or
- protect the rights, security, and safety of Healthofast, our users, or others.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by Healthofast. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are also responsible for protecting access to your device and any account credentials you use with Healthofast.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain synchronized account data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

If you want to request deletion of your account or synchronized data, contact us using the privacy contact information provided below. We may need to verify your request before processing it.

Data stored only on your device can generally be removed by deleting the relevant Healthofast data or uninstalling the app, subject to your device's operating-system behavior.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Healthofast does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us in a way that violates applicable law, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Healthofast after an update means the updated policy applies to your use of the app, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
